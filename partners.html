Partner programmes

Two ways to become a partner

Every ARIANNA partner joins as an implementation partner, a reseller partner, or both.

Implementation partners

Security consultants specialised in vulnerability management, product security and regulatory compliance. They provide:

  • Design and improvement of vulnerability management processes
  • Integration of ARIANNA into existing development and security workflows
  • Preparation for the CRA, RED and other regulations
  • Complementary services, such as cybersecurity consultancy, including penetration testing, and holistic compliance assessments

Reseller partners

Partners who make ARIANNA available in their region and industry. They provide:

  • Local sales and onboarding support
  • Product demonstrations and technical guidance
  • Licensing advice
  • First-line customer support
