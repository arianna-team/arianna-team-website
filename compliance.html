Cyber Resilience Act · Regulation (EU) 2024/2847

What the CRA requires, and how ARIANNA does it

01

Know every component

Each device holds hundreds of third-party software components. ARIANNA builds an exact SBOM for every firmware version with proprietary SCA tools: microcontroller firmware, embedded Linux, Windows, cloud and mobile apps. No binaries or source code are shared.

02

Monitor vulnerabilities for 5+ years

Third-party components can introduce security vulnerabilities into your products. ARIANNA checks every component daily against public vulnerabilities databases, including NIST NVD, CVE.org, GitHub Security Advisories and ENISA's EUVD.

03

Fix without delay

ARIANNA shows a prioritised list per product and version, with known patches and mitigations. Remediation deadlines follow your vulnerability management policy, with a reminder before each one is due.

04

Report and keep evidence

ARIANNA records every triage decision and status change as an auditable history, flags known exploited vulnerabilities, and exports reports as PDF, CSV, CycloneDX, SPDX and VEX.

The CRA applies to products with digital elements sold in the EU. Fines reach €15M or 2.5% of global turnover, and non-compliant products lose CE marking. Harmonised standards are being developed as the EN 40000-1-x series.

Radio Equipment Directive

RED cybersecurity, in force since August 2025

The RED Delegated Act (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30, amended by (EU) 2023/2444) adds cybersecurity requirements for in-scope radio equipment placed on the EU market, from Wi-Fi and Bluetooth devices to anything that connects to the internet.

  • Vulnerability assessment evidence. Show that known vulnerabilities in your radio products' components have been identified and assessed.
  • Risk management. Document applicability, accepted risks and mitigations, backed by security claims.
  • A head start on the CRA. The same Device Model and process carry over when the CRA fully applies.

IEC 62443

The bar in industrial markets

Industrial and energy customers increasingly require suppliers to follow IEC 62443. Its secure development lifecycle (62443-4-1) expects defined processes for handling security issues and managing security updates.

  • A documented process. ARIANNA's four-stage pipeline gives every vulnerability a consistent, auditable path.
  • Policy-based deadlines. Remediation timeframes by risk level, with expiring and overdue items highlighted.
  • Evidence for assessors. Full action history and exports for audits and customer reviews.

Medical devices

SBOMs and vulnerability evidence for FDA and MDR

Medical regulators now expect manufacturers to list the software in their devices and to show how its vulnerabilities are monitored and handled after release. ARIANNA produces the SBOM and keeps the vulnerability record for submissions and post-market surveillance.
