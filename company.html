About ARIANNA

Built by product security experts, for device manufacturers

ARIANNA Team is an Italian technology company. We build the ARIANNA platform to help manufacturers of connected devices identify, assess and manage vulnerabilities throughout the product life cycle, and to make product security lean, efficient and sustainable.

Our story

Born inside an embedded security consultancy

ARIANNA was built inside Security Pattern, an Italian consultancy company specialised in cybersecurity for embedded systems. Years of hands-on vulnerability assessments and CRA / RED compliance work for device manufacturers showed the same gap again and again: no tool was designed for the way connected products are actually built and maintained.

So the team built one. In 2026 ARIANNA became its own company, ARIANNA Team s.r.l., with Security Pattern remaining a shareholder and partner.

Milestones

  1. 2023–2026ARIANNA is developed inside Security Pattern, side by side with its users, growing from 3 customers in 2023 to more than 40 in 2025.
  2. 2026First international partners in the Benelux and Asia.
  3. Aug 2026Spin-off as ARIANNA Team s.r.l.
