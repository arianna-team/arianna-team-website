Why We Built ARIANNA

A Better Operating Model

We believe vulnerability management for connected products should be:

  • Continuous across the full product lifecycle
  • Grounded in accurate SBOM and HBOM context
  • Traceable enough for audits, customers, and internal governance
  • Designed for embedded, OT, and regulated environments, not just enterprise IT

Our goal is to help teams manage cybersecurity risk systematically, with a process that survives handoffs between engineering, security, quality, and compliance.

What We Deliver

ARIANNA gives product teams a practical system for identifying, prioritizing, tracking, and reporting vulnerabilities. We help organizations:

  • Build trustworthy software and hardware inventories
  • Monitor vulnerability exposure across complex product supply chains
  • Prioritize remediation using exploitability and product context
  • Support secure development, triage, and post-market response workflows
  • Generate audit-ready evidence without extra manual reporting layers

The emphasis is on operational clarity: what is affected, what matters now, who owns the next step, and how the decision will be documented.

Built Around Real Product Environments

ARIANNA is designed for sectors where reliability, safety, and traceability matter: automotive, industrial automation, medical devices, energy, transportation, defense, and connected consumer products.

These environments have long support windows, complex supplier relationships, certification constraints, and different stakeholders involved in every security decision. The platform is built around those realities.

How We Work With Customers

We focus on giving teams a usable operating model, not just another data source. That means clear workflows, explainable prioritization, and evidence that stands up to customer and regulatory scrutiny.

Our commitment is to help customers make vulnerability management repeatable, accountable, and sustainable over the full product lifecycle.
