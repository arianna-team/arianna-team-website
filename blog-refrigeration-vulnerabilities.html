The refrigeration systems behind the attacks: known vulnerabilities, ignored security advisories?

Which refrigeration controllers were targeted in the October 2026 attacks, which vulnerabilities were already known for them, and which security advisories had been published.

Known vulnerabilities31 CVEs

Copeland / Dixell

Dixell is a product brand within Copeland, a global manufacturer of heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration (HVACR) technologies. Its portfolio includes a wide range of electronic control and monitoring solutions for refrigeration systems, such as:

  • Electronic temperature controllers
  • Compressor and fan controllers
  • Electronic expansion valve drivers
  • Sensors and monitoring systems
  • XWEB remote supervision and monitoring systems

Copeland's solutions are used across several sectors, including:

  • Supermarkets and food retail refrigeration
  • Industrial refrigeration and cold storage
  • Refrigerated transportation and cold-chain logistics
  • Commercial and residential HVAC systems

XWEB: remote supervision and monitoring

XWEB systems are particularly relevant to the recent cyberattacks. They provide centralized monitoring and management of refrigeration installations, allowing operators and technicians to monitor equipment, receive alarms and remotely manage system parameters.

As a result, these systems are an important part of the operational technology (OT) infrastructure of supermarkets and other refrigeration-dependent facilities.

The vulnerabilities include multiple instances of OS command injection, potentially allowing authenticated attackers to execute arbitrary operating-system commands. Other issues include pre-authentication code execution, authentication bypass, buffer overflow and information disclosure.

Danfoss

Danfoss is a Danish multinational engineering company that develops technologies for refrigeration, air conditioning, heating, industrial automation, hydraulics and energy efficiency.

Its Climate Solutions business is particularly relevant to refrigeration and cold-chain applications. It provides components and complete control solutions used by supermarkets, food processing facilities, cold storage warehouses and industrial refrigeration plants.

AK-SM & AK-SC

The AK-SM (System Manager) is a central management system for supermarket refrigeration and HVAC installations. It coordinates connected controllers, collects temperature and operating data, manages alarms, and supports the supervision and optimization of refrigeration functions.

The AK-SC (System Controller) family comprises controllers used to manage and supervise refrigeration installations.

Security warnings that may have gone unheeded

Manufacturers issued security advisories well before the attacks reported in October 2026. These warnings highlighted known vulnerabilities and recommended protective measures, including firmware updates and restrictions on remote access. The incidents raise the question of whether these recommendations were adequately implemented.

  1. August 2023, March 2025 & August 2025Danfoss security advisories

    Danfoss published security advisories concerning vulnerabilities in its AK-SM 8xxA systems. All advisories recommended installing the latest available software versions to address the reported vulnerabilities.

    • DSA-2023-08-01 · August 2023
    • DSA-2025-03-01 · March 2025
    • DSA-2025-08-01 · August 2025
  2. February 2026Copeland security bulletin

    Copeland published a security bulletin addressing vulnerabilities in its XWEB PRO monitoring systems. The bulletin recommended updating the firmware and restricting direct Internet exposure to reduce the risk of exploitation.

    • XWEB PRO Security Update · February 2026

    In October 2026, Dixell also published security recommendations for the safe deployment and operation of XWEB monitoring systems.

    • Recommendations for the Secure Use of XWEB Supervisors · October 2026
  3. October 2026CERT-FR alert

    Following reports of compromised refrigeration systems, France's national cybersecurity agency, CERT-FR, published an alert identifying affected Danfoss AK-SM and AK-SC controllers.

    The alert recommended removing direct Internet exposure and restricting remote access to these systems.

    Importantly, CERT-FR did not establish that the attacks exploited the CVEs listed above. The initial attack vector remains unconfirmed.

    • Alert CERTFR-2026-ALE-012 · October 2026

A recurring warning

The timeline shows that manufacturers had disclosed vulnerabilities and issued security recommendations well before the incidents became public.

The central question is therefore not only whether vulnerabilities were known, but whether affected systems were updated, properly configured and adequately protected against external access.
